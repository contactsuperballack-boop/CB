Questions

Quick answers

How long does a project take?

Short ads and animations usually take one to three weeks. Branding usually takes two to four weeks. Exact timing is confirmed in the quote.

How much does a project cost?

Pricing is on request. Send a short brief and you receive a quote after a review of scope.

What is in the System Prompt Library?

Master workflow prompts for 15 animation styles, such as claymation, paper cut, anime and Ghibli, cyberpunk and explainer videos, plus packs for product traction, premium product ads and UGC, branding, product film studio and informercials.

Can I use it for client work?

Yes, for your own projects and your clients’ projects. You may not resell, share or republish the library itself.
