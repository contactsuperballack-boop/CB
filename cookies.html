Last updated 14 November 2026. Plain-language draft, not legal advice. Have a lawyer review it before relying on it.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising trackers.

Third-party content

Fonts load from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when a page loads. The map of Abuja loads from OpenStreetMap only if you click it. The Paystack checkout page, which opens when you buy the library, sets its own cookies under Paystack’s policy. Links to WhatsApp, Instagram and YouTube are governed by those services’ own policies once you open them.
